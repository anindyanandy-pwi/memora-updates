# Memora — privacy policy

*Alpha · November 2026 · contact: memoralabs.inc@gmail.com*

Memora is a personal memory app for your phone. It keeps the messages and files that reach you, and helps you find them again by searching in plain words.

## What stays on your phone
Everything Memora keeps stays on your phone and is never uploaded by Memora:
- messages, emails, voice notes, notes, files and photos;
- the text read from photos and documents, and the text of voice notes;
- your searches, your Today list and what you mark done, snoozed or not a task.

Messages, text and your searches are kept in Memora's own encrypted database; its key is kept in your phone's secure hardware. Copies of files, photos and voice notes are kept in Memora's private app storage, which other apps cannot open and which Android encrypts with your phone's lock. None of it is included in Google's backup or copied to a new phone; Memora's own backup file is the way to move it.

**Message notifications.** Memora reads the notifications of your chat, SMS and email apps (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Messenger, Messages, Gmail and Outlook), and of messages other apps mark as a chat or an email: the sender, the chat or subject, the message text and attached files. One-time passwords (OTPs) are never kept, and card and Aadhaar numbers are hidden. For all other apps Memora does not read what a notification says; it only notes that one arrived, to check that capture still works. You can choose chats Memora skips, and forget what it kept from a chat.

**Folders you choose.** Memora reads photos and documents only from the folders you pick (for example Camera, Screenshots, Documents and WhatsApp's media folder). It does not ask to see all your photos or all your files. You can remove a folder at any time in your phone's settings.

**Voice notes and memos** are turned into text on your phone. The audio and the text never leave it.

## What can leave your phone, only if you turn it on
- **Weekly usage count** (off by default). Once a week it sends:
  - whether you used Memora;
  - roughly how many searches;
  - whether capture is working;
  - your phone brand and Android version;
  - a random number made on your phone.

  No messages, names, contacts, files or search words. Turning it off deletes the random number. IP addresses are not stored.
- **Sync with your own PC** (if you pair one): your memories travel encrypted between your own devices.
- **Backup file** (if you make one, or turn on the weekly backup): it is saved only to the place or folder you choose (your PC, a pen drive or your own cloud drive's folder), locked with a 24-word recovery code that only you have. Memora keeps only a key that can lock backups, never one that can open them. Memora never uploads a backup.
- **Reports you send:** a problem report, a crash report or a performance report goes only if you send it yourself from your email app, after you have seen everything in it. Crash reports contain technical details only.

## Downloads
Memora downloads its search and voice-to-text models once, from its public release page. Nothing about you is sent.

## Permissions
- **Notification access:** to remember messages (explained on the screen before you turn it on).
- **Folders you pick:** to read photos and documents in them.
- **Microphone:** only while you record a voice memo.
- **Contacts** (optional): to show names instead of numbers. Read on the phone only, never stored outside it.
- **Camera:** to take a photo for Memora.
- **Display over other apps:** for the Memora Orb.
- **Notifications:** for the morning briefing and to tell you if capture stops (both silent).

## Children
Memora is not meant for children under 13.
